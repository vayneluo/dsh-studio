# Provider onboarding notices

The onboarding interface names third-party model providers solely to help users
select and configure a service they already use. Provider names and associated
trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Their appearance
does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation.

The interface does not bundle provider logos or other third-party artwork.
Each provider is represented by a locally rendered text monogram and a generic
color treatment. No remote images, fonts, scripts, analytics, or tracking
resources are loaded by the onboarding override.
